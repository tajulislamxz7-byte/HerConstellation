Privacy policy

This site has no accounts, sign-in, or comments for visitors.

What is collected

When you tap a download button, the site adds one to that app’s download count. The count is not linked to your name or identity. Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operation.

Cookies and storage

Visitors are not given tracking cookies. The site may store its own files in your browser so pages load faster and work offline.

Downloads from other sites

Some APK files are hosted on other services such as cloud storage. Those services have their own privacy policies. App icons and screenshots may also be loaded from other sites, which can see your IP address and device details when your browser requests them. Your browser does not tell them which page you came from.
